Our Story

Where Coal Meets Culinary Art

Now Serving at 3 Locations

Koila — meaning "coal" in Nepali — is born from the idea that fire transforms simple ingredients into extraordinary experiences. Our chefs master the flame, bringing you dishes that are smoky, sizzling, and unforgettable.

We blend traditional Nepali flavors with global cuisines, offering everything from authentic Khaja Sets and Mo:Mo to wood-fired pizzas, sizzlers, and Indo-Chinese classics. With three outlets across Kathmandu, we bring the warmth of the hearth to your table. Every dish is prepared with locally sourced ingredients, bold spices, and a touch of fire.
